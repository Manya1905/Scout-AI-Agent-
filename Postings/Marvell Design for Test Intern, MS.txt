Your Team, Your Impact

The Compute and Custom Solutions (CCS) Business Unit within Marvell develops cutting-edge SoCs and processors in advanced process nodes for some of the largest companies in the world. We develop silicon solutions for the growing data center, enterprise, wireless, and automotive compute markets, collaborating with strategic customers from architecture and design all the way through layout, packaging, prototype validation, and production ramp-up.
The CCS DFT (Design for Test) team is a key part of Marvell's global DFT community, with global ownership and responsibility for delivering generic and more advanced custom DFT architecture solutions, methodology, and design. The team provides technology development, EDA/methodology development, and IP/chip design development. As an intern, you will be working with this team to directly enable customer DFT requirements in the CCS BU, gaining hands-on experience with industry-leading tools and methodologies used to build some of the world's most complex SoCs.
What You Can Expect

Develop understanding of the block level or chip top DFT and pattern generation flows for complex SOC designs
Execute DFT insertion, pattern generation and verification flows for Scan, Memory Built-in Self-Test (MBIST), Boundary Scan and IP macro test
Run DFT checks and analyze results to resolve testability issues and improve DFT coverage
Support DFT flow automation through scripting (Tcl, Python, or shell) to improve design flows
Contribute to DFT documentation, methodology improvements and automation
Present intern project results to the team at the end of the internship
What We're Looking For

Currently pursuing a Masters in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or related fields  
Good understanding of digital logic design using Verilog/System Verilog
Knowledge of DFT concepts, JTAG standards and Boundary scan implementation
Knowledge of Scan test, memory BIST/BISR, functional test, JTAG, and other test methodologies is a plus
Experience in programming and scripting languages such as Python, Shell, Tcl
Knowledge of using AI assisted tools for coding, scripts and automation is a plus
Effective teamwork and communication skills
